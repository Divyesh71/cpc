Treatments/Spinal Cord Stimulation

Spinal Cord Stimulation

Advanced Neuromodulation for Chronic Pain

Spinal cord stimulation is an advanced form of neuromodulation that uses mild electrical stimulation to modify pain signals traveling through the nervous system.

For appropriately selected patients with certain chronic neuropathic pain conditions, spinal cord stimulation may help reduce pain and improve function.

How It Works

How Does SCS Work?

Spinal cord stimulation uses mild electrical stimulation to modify pain signals traveling through the nervous system.

  1. 01

    Leads Are Positioned

    Thin electrical leads are positioned within the epidural space near the spinal cord.

  2. 02

    Programmed Stimulation

    The leads deliver carefully programmed electrical impulses designed to modify pain signaling.

The SCS Trial and Permanent Implantation

Stage 1

The SCS Trial

One important feature of spinal cord stimulation is the ability to evaluate the therapy before permanent implantation.

  • Temporary leads are placed and connected to an external stimulation system.
  • The patient’s response is evaluated before deciding whether permanent implantation should be considered.
Stage 2

Permanent Implantation

Patients who experience satisfactory benefit during the trial may be considered for implantation of a permanent stimulation system.

  • The device can subsequently be programmed and adjusted according to the patient’s response.

Who May Be a Candidate?

SCS may be considered for appropriately selected patients with certain chronic neuropathic pain conditions who have not obtained sufficient improvement from appropriate conservative treatment. Every patient requires an individualized evaluation.
