# California Pain Consultants — Website Modernization

## Project objective
Complete modernization and redesign of the California Pain Consultants website. Not just cosmetic: the site must accurately represent the practice as a comprehensive, advanced **Interventional Pain Medicine** practice specializing in modern minimally invasive treatments for chronic spine, nerve, joint and musculoskeletal pain.

The site must immediately communicate:
- Advanced Interventional Pain Medicine
- Neuromodulation
- Minimally Invasive Spine Procedures
- Image-Guided Interventional Procedures
- Regenerative Medicine
- Biopsychosocial Pain Management

Goals: modern, sophisticated, professional, medically credible, patient-friendly, mobile-friendly, fast, easy to navigate, easy for referring physicians to use, optimized for local search, designed to convert appropriate visitors into consultation requests.

## CRITICAL RULES
1. **Do NOT redesign the live website directly.** Build a complete STAGING website first.
2. Staging must be reviewed and approved before it replaces the existing website.
3. **Do not delete** existing pages, URLs, documents, SEO settings, redirects, analytics, tracking codes or forms until the migration plan has been approved.

## Brand positioning
Must NOT look like: a medication-management clinic, a generic injection clinic, a spine surgery practice, a physical therapy office, a med spa, or a retail regenerative medicine clinic.

Must look like a sophisticated Interventional Pain Medicine center. Visual impression:
Advanced Medicine · Precision · Technology · Experience · Compassion · Trust · Restoring Function

### Our Approach — "Treating the Whole Person, Not Just the Pain"
Chronic pain is influenced by more than anatomy alone.

Pain can affect movement, sleep, physical activity, work, emotional well-being, relationships and overall quality of life.

California Pain Consultants uses a comprehensive biopsychosocial and multimodal approach to chronic pain.

Treatment begins with understanding the patient's symptoms, identifying potential pain generators and evaluating how pain affects daily function and quality of life.

Depending upon the individual patient, treatment may incorporate education, physical rehabilitation, lifestyle strategies, appropriate medications, targeted injections, minimally invasive procedures, regenerative therapies, neuromodulation and other advanced interventional treatments.

Our objective is to develop an individualized treatment strategy designed to reduce pain, restore function and help patients participate more fully in their lives.
