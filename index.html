Our Approach

Treating the Whole Person, Not Just the Pain

Chronic pain is influenced by more than anatomy alone.

Pain can affect movement, sleep, physical activity, work, emotional well-being, relationships and overall quality of life.

Our Objective

Our objective is to develop an individualized treatment strategy designed to reduce pain, restore function and help patients participate more fully in their lives.

  1. 01

    Comprehensive & Multimodal Care

    California Pain Consultants uses a comprehensive biopsychosocial and multimodal approach to chronic pain.

  2. 02

    Understand & Evaluate

    Treatment begins with understanding the patient’s symptoms, identifying potential pain generators and evaluating how pain affects daily function and quality of life.

  3. 03

    Personalized Treatment Plan

    Depending upon the individual patient, treatment may incorporate education, physical rehabilitation, lifestyle strategies, appropriate medications, targeted injections, minimally invasive procedures, regenerative therapies, neuromodulation and other advanced interventional treatments.
